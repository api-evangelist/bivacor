# BiVACOR

> BiVACOR, Inc. is a privately held, clinical-stage medical device company developing the BiVACOR Total Artificial Heart (TAH) — a titanium biventricular rotary blood pump with a single magnetically levitated rotor and a dual-sided impeller that replaces both ventricles of a failing heart. It has corporate operations in Huntington Beach, California, clinical operations in Houston, Texas, and engineering operations in Southport, Queensland, Australia. Founder and Chief Technical Officer Daniel Timms, PhD; CEO Jim Dillon; Chief Medical Officer William E. Cohn, MD; Board Chairman Raymond W. Cohen.

BiVACOR is a medical hardware and clinical-research business, not a software vendor. As of 2026-08-07 it publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no API documentation, no SDK and no machine-readable API contract**. Its web presence is a single-page WordPress marketing site. Do not assume or invent an `api.bivacor.com`, `docs.bivacor.com`, `developer.bivacor.com`, `portal.bivacor.com`, `status.bivacor.com` or `trust.bivacor.com` endpoint — none of those hosts resolve in DNS. `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/graphql`, `/llms.txt`, `/.well-known/security.txt`, `/.well-known/api-catalog`, `/.well-known/agent-card.json` and `/.well-known/agent.json` all return 404 on bivacor.com.

`https://bivacor.com/wp-json/` does return 200 — but that is the stock WordPress REST API that ships enabled by default with the CMS and its plugins, not a product API BiVACOR designed, documented or offers to integrators. It is deliberately **not** registered as an API in this profile and must not be described as BiVACOR's API.

The BiVACOR Total Artificial Heart is an **investigational device**, limited by federal law to investigational use, and is **not approved for commercial sale in any geography**. Statements about it should be read as investigational, not commercial.

## Technology

The BiVACOR TAH is an electro-mechanical rotary blood pump rather than a volume-displacement pump. A single rotor with a dual-sided impeller is held in magnetic levitation (MAGLEV), pumping simultaneously to the systemic and pulmonary circulations. Consequences of that design, as the company describes it:

- No valves, no flexing ventricular chambers or pumping diaphragms, and no mechanical bearings — so no contact wear surfaces
- Flows above 12 L/min, enough to support exercise
- Pulsatile flow generated by cyclic modulation of rotor speed
- A patented left/right flow-balancing mechanism for hemodynamic adaptation
- A smart controller that adapts pump output to patient activity
- Titanium construction for biocompatibility and durability
- Sized for most adults with a body surface area above 1.4 m²
- Portable external controller with rechargeable batteries

## Regulatory and clinical status

- FDA Investigational Device Exemption (IDE) approval for a first-in-human Early Feasibility Study, announced 2023-11-29 — [press release](https://bivacor.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/US-FDA-Grants-the-BiVACOR-Total-Artificial-Heart-IDE-Approval-for-First-in-Human-Early-Feasibility-Study.pdf)
- First-in-human implantation 2024-07-09 at The Texas Heart Institute / Baylor St. Luke's Medical Center — [announcement](https://bivacor.com/the-texas-heart-institute-implants-bivacor-total-artificial-heart/)
- All five initial EFS patients bridged to a donor heart transplant; FDA approved expansion to 15 more patients, announced 2024-12-18
- First Australian implantation 2024-11-22 at St Vincent's Hospital Sydney, with the patient supported for over 100 days — [press release](https://bivacor.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/BiVACOR-First-TAH-in-Australia.pdf)
- FDA Breakthrough Device Designation, announced 2025-05-30
- Accepted into the FDA Total Product Life Cycle Advisory Program (TAP), announced 2025-08-06
- No FDA premarket authorization; the device is not approved for commercial sale
- [BiVACOR Total Artificial Heart Early Feasibility Study, NCT06174103](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06174103): RECRUITING, started 2024-06-26
